## Core Playstyle

Aaron strongly favors:

- **Close or midfield combat**
- Strong audiovisual **impact / gravity**
- Big payoff attacks
- Burst windows
- Jobs with strong identities
- Complexity when it produces an obvious payoff
- Active gameplay rather than complexity for complexity's sake

Aaron tends not to enjoy:

- Setup that feels like temporarily not attacking
- Weak audiovisual feedback
- Mechanics that feel busy without a satisfying payoff

A useful shorthand is that Aaron likes jobs where the buttons **feel like they matter**.

## Current Favorites / Main Contenders

### Tanks

**2026-11-10 playstyle update:** Gunbreaker is increasingly a *main-tank* preference, not just DPS-flavored off-tanking. Aaron voluntarily calls MT and other tanks defer to him; boss positioning, cast-bar monitoring, mitigation and ground mechanics make fights more engaging than pure DPS throughput. Continuation at level 70 significantly improved the fun and perceived damage output. Current Omega clears are tanked on GNB for faster queues; plans to revisit on Reaper.

#### Dark Knight

- Major favorite and long-term main contender.
- Current level: **63**.
- Enjoys the heavy greatsword feel, strong job fantasy, and the sense that every attack has weight.
- Described tanking as feeling like **"DPS with better defense."**
- DRK has worked especially well as an MSQ job.

#### Gunbreaker

- Major favorite after initially getting a weaker first impression.
- Current level: **70**.
- Current appeal is the **DPS-like burst-window structure**, cartridge flow, and busy technical feel, plus meaningful encounter responsibility as main tank. Continuation especially clicked at level 70.
- Aaron has compared its complexity favorably to MCH.
- Wicked Talon / later burst-style attacks strongly fit Aaron's preference for obvious payoff.
- DRK and GNB are currently the two preferred tanks.
- Recent playtime has heavily favored **GNB + MCH**.

#### Warrior

- Historically a favorite tank on the older character.
- Current job level: **Warrior 57**; Warrior is now unlocked on this character.
- Strong appeal: **Fell Cleave**, extreme self-sustain, simple aggression, and the "unga bunga" power fantasy.
- Progressed from Marauder 15 to Warrior 57 by the 2026-10-22 snapshot; no new gameplay preference verdict was supplied.

#### Paladin

- Current experiment rather than a favorite.
- Current level: **63**.
- Early GLA/PLD felt **slow and boring**.
- Later holy-sword / giant-radiant-blade visuals and BG3-style Divine Smite fantasy renewed motivation to continue.
- Now level 63; no updated gameplay verdict was supplied with the latest level snapshot.

### Melee DPS

#### Samurai

- Major favorite and one of the standards against which other jobs are judged.
- Current level: **63**.
- Core appeal: close combat, constant contact, Sen buildup, Midare payoff, excellent sound/animation weight.
- Aaron's recurring summary: **"SAM just fucks."**

#### Ninja

- **Level 90 via job boost** as of the start of Stormblood (`Beyond the Great Wall`).
- Now an active evaluation candidate rather than merely a future possibility.
- Strongly likes the Japanese/shinobi fantasy and close-combat style.
- Main historical friction: mudra inputs felt like **not doing damage while setting up the attack**.
- Recent enjoyment of GNB/MCH burst windows makes NIN especially worth a serious current-version trial.
- Do not assume the boost means NIN has become a favorite; Aaron has not yet given a post-boost gameplay verdict.

### Physical Ranged

#### Machinist

- Rapidly became a major favorite.
- Current level: **70**.
- **Drill** was the turning point; Reassemble + Drill is especially satisfying.
- Likes the job's industrial, high-impact attacks and burst-window management.
- Often plays MCH in the **midfield**, reflecting a general preference for proximity even on ranged jobs.
- Current sentiment is effectively: **MCH kinda fucks now.**
- Recent playtime has heavily favored **MCH + GNB**.

#### Dancer

- Current level: **60**.
- Initially felt too busy when introduced with many abilities at once.
- Felt much better when played synced at a sensible level.
- Positive, but currently behind MCH in physical-ranged preference.

### Healers

#### Scholar

- Current level: **60**.
- Established favorite healer.
- Enjoys proactive shields, mitigation, planning, and tactical payoff.
- Scratches an "engineer" / encounter-solving itch.

#### Astrologian

- Current level: **63**.
- Established favorite healer.
- Enjoys HoTs, buffs, dynamic healing, and clutch recovery situations.
- Healing is memorable because messy pulls create stories rather than just rotations.

#### Sage

- Current level: **70**.
- Unlocked and at level **70**; hands-on detailed verdict not yet reported.
- Expected appeal: aggressive barrier healing, mobility, lasers, planning, and a more active presentation of the things Aaron enjoys about SCH.

### Future Jobs of Interest

- **Reaper** — now unlocked and level **70**; loves its priority/burst feel, avoiding rote 1-2-3 combos, visual impact and heavy attacks; ranks close to Samurai. Plans to revisit Omega raids as RPR.
- **Viper** — expected to fit the desire for fast, close, continuous combat without NIN's mudra friction.
- **Pictomancer** — curiosity remains; must pass the "oomph" test rather than feel like weak audiovisual pixie dust.

## Jobs That Did Not Click

### Red Mage

- Current level: **50**.
- Did not click.
- Casting itself was not the main problem.
- Black/white mana balancing was not compelling.
- Melee combo was the favorite part.
- Main criticism was weak audiovisual impact: spells felt like **"throwing pixie dust."**

### Black Mage / Thaumaturge

- Current class level: **Thaumaturge 4**.
- Early experience felt boring.
- Later BLM may still theoretically fit the desire for heavy spell impact, but caster gameplay is currently less natural than melee/tank/MCH play.

## Role Philosophy

- Tanking has become a major focus; Aaron spent several recent play sessions mostly tanking.
- **DRK + GNB** remain favored tanks; GNB is currently dominant and has become a main-tanking favorite.
- **GNB + MCH** have dominated the most recent playtime, suggesting a strong preference for structured, mechanical burst loops with obvious payoff.
- WAR is the likely "immortal unga bunga" alternate when leveled again.
- PLD is being given a fair trial to see whether its later holy-knight identity overcomes the dull early levels.
- Aaron increasingly prefers main-tanking for encounter ownership and engagement, not merely faster queues or durability. DPS remains enjoyable for alternative perspectives and rotations.
- Healing remains appealing for its improvisational / recovery gameplay even though tanks have recently dominated playtime.
